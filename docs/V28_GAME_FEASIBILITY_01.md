# V2.8 game-feasibility probe 01

Date: 2026-09-30. This is a small model-blind engineering probe, not a game-strength, rules-certification, training, or JEPA result. The compact source-hashed receipt is [V28_GAME_FEASIBILITY_01.json](validation/V28_GAME_FEASIBILITY_01.json); per-root output is excluded at `chess_data/two-player-klent-toy/v28_connect4_4x5_feas_dev01.json`.

The existing project oracle-cost probe sampled 24 fixed Connect4 4x5 **without gravity** trajectories at target plies 5–11, with a 100,000-node and one-second cap per root. Twenty-three roots were nonterminal; 12/23 received complete exact action-value maps and 11/23 timed out. Eleven of the 12 completed roots had more than one terminal-outcome label among legal actions. Exact labels and positions were not retained by the probe, no model predictions were used, and no data or training were generated.

This completion rate makes that no-gravity variant a poor default candidate for the exact-regret bank under this budget. The sample is too small to estimate general solvability; do not compensate by keeping only easy completed roots. A separate earlier model-blind receipt for **gravity** Connect4 4x5 at target plies 5–11 completed 22/22 nonterminal roots and found 17 with varying action outcomes under the same one-second cap (`V26_ORACLE_FEASIBILITY_04_CONNECT4_GRAVITY.json`). The V2 survey03 bank also passed its earlier support floors for gravity Connect4 4x5 and Reversi6 (500 admitted roots each, 195 and 499 beyond-depth, respectively), but those banks belong to prior development and are exposed; they cannot serve as V2.8 locked confirmation.

Tentative game-selection decision: investigate fresh, independently split banks for gravity Connect4 4x5 and Reversi6, with Reversi6 first rechecked for exact-regret label coverage at the V2.8 root schedule. Neither is admitted as a scientific V2.8 game until a fresh 100-unique-root/50-beyond-depth support audit, zero-overlap trajectory/counterfactual audit, opponent-bank calibration, and model-blind power analysis pass. The no-gravity Connect4 4x5 variant is deferred from the primary gate unless a newly predeclared compute/sampling design improves coverage without filtering hard roots.

This result does not establish that the proposed game selection is novel, difficult enough for learned models, or representative of the target class. Reversi4 remains a software/split-pipeline fixture only.
