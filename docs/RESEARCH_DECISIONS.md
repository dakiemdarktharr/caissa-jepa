# MARS-JEPA Chess research decisions

Current identity and methodology are defined in [RESEARCH_IDENTITY.md](RESEARCH_IDENTITY.md)
and [protocol version 2](../V7_RESEARCH_PROTOCOL.md). The earlier three-JEPA
roster is superseded by the seven entries in `model_registry.py`.

H1/H2 and no-response are active tested ablations. The response aggregation is
an uncalibrated learned-policy expectation. LeJEPA-inspired and NNUE-style remain
local adaptations with no equivalence claim to their external research namesakes.

The production dataset was intentionally removed. Historical measurements and
pipeline-recovery receipts cannot establish validity of present checkpoints or
confirmatory results. Refer to [hardening limitations](MARS_HARDENING.md).
