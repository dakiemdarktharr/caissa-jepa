# Method V2.8-P v0.5 amendment: paired match estimand after exact-label failure

**Status: frozen pre-fit redesign candidate; independent methods review signed off, not trained or confirmed.** Read with V0.1–V0.4. This amendment is triggered by the model-blind DEV10 result and supersedes the exact-root minimax-regret primary endpoint in V0.1–V0.4. It retains those versions and their receipts as historical evidence. No learned outcome has been observed.

## Why the estimand changed

DEV10 evaluated five fixed-schedule gravity Connect4 6x7 roots at plies 5–11. The independent rule comparison passed for all five, but complete exact action-value maps were not returned for any root under 500,000 nodes, a 500,000-entry cache, and two seconds per root. This exploratory pilot is too small to estimate full gate coverage; it establishes only that this budget did not solve these five roots. It does not show that exact 6x7 labels are intrinsically infeasible under other root schedules or compute budgets. The pilot receipt is `docs/validation/V28_ROOT_ORACLE_DEV10_PILOT.json`.

The primary endpoint is therefore paired head-to-head match score, computed from complete games and requiring no exact root-value oracle. Exact minimax root regret may remain a secondary analysis only on a separately frozen subset for which all scheduled roots are solved; unresolved roots remain reported and cannot be silently removed. This redesign does not make finite-opponent results evidence of equilibrium, exploitability, optimal-play strength, or response prediction.

## Method and matched estimand

The candidate remains the shared two-game reply-set JEPA specified in V0.1–V0.4: for every enumerated legal two-ply branch, a predictor receives the root latent and the ordered current-player action and opponent-reply action, then predicts an EMA target latent at the resulting state. The exact adapter supplies legal actions and terminal outcomes. A fixed two-ply max-min backup uses a learned value at nonterminal leaves. It models a worst-case legal responder in search; the logged self-play policy families are data-collection behaviors, not opponent predictions.

For each game, fixed checkpoint seed, and paired match seed, run a JEPA-versus-baseline pair with player colors swapped. Score a win as 1, draw as 0.5, and loss as 0 for JEPA. The primary contrast is the paired score statistic defined in protocol V08 against each of the two strongest preregistered same-search non-JEPA controls: task-value latent dynamics and direct exact-leaf value. The primary inference is conditional on the frozen checkpoint panel and uses paired match-seed uncertainty; seed-to-seed variation is reported separately and a hierarchical seed analysis is a sensitivity analysis, not population-level training-seed inference. Use a game-balanced aggregate fixed before training, retain per-game effects, and correct the two primary comparisons with Holm at family-wise alpha 0.05. The +0.05 practical score margin and guardrails are defined in V08. Report all outcomes, draws, model timeouts/forfeits, and external censored blocks under fixed rules.

The fixed secondary suite includes project-owned uniform-legal, tactical, positional, and bounded-search agents. These are finite reference opponents only. Report per-opponent and color-swapped scores; do not call them exploitability, equilibrium, minimax regret, or behavior-model accuracy. Whole-game outcomes are separate from all-legal ranking, value error, latent prediction error, collapse, calibration, and compute diagnostics.

## Fairness and falsification controls

All learned arms use the same trajectory IDs and observed labels, encoder capacity, policy/value targets, optimizer/update schedule, training seeds, environment steps, data order, game adapter, terminal handling, search code, roots, tie-breaking schedule, and inference wall-clock/node budgets. The JEPA arm alone receives the latent target-matching term. The task-value dynamics arm has the same predictor and search interface but no latent-state target loss. The direct-leaf arm encodes exact two-ply leaf observations using the same encoder/value capacity but has no learned transition predictor. Report both equal-update and equal-measured-compute results; charge predictor, target, encoder, rule-transition, and search costs to the appropriate arm. Include alpha-beta/reference search at its own fixed budget as a nonlearned ceiling/control, with version and source hash pinned.

No arm may train on match outcomes from the confirmatory schedule. Development matches, opponent identities, seeds, and checkpoint selection are disjoint from locked confirmation. Freeze the locked schedule and evaluator source hash before any training; publish its commitment hash without exposing outcomes until all model/config hashes are committed. A model-specific timeout or compute-budget overrun is part of the bounded-compute outcome and counts as a forfeit/loss under the frozen score rule; it cannot censor a difficult block. Only external infrastructure failures that affect every compared arm may censor a paired block, and the reason/count must be reported. Illegal moves are losses. Any rule mismatch, incorrect replay, or nondeterministic replay halts the full evaluation and invalidates that study version; retain all raw outputs, fix the implementation under a new version, and generate a new locked schedule before any confirmatory rerun. Do not selectively censor or rerun blocks.

## Entry gates and claim ceiling

Before development fitting, pass independent rule-transition tests for both admitted games, strict DEV09 data and provenance audit, train/validation/selection group separation, opponent and seat coverage, a match-schedule smoke test with only nonlearned agents, compute measurement, and independent method/fairness review. Estimate power using a disclosed range of paired effects and a model-blind nonlearned schedule; state clearly that this is scenario-based planning, not observed JEPA effect. If the prespecified practical effect cannot reach 80% power within the declared local-compute and match cap, narrow the primary claim or stop rather than using unapproved cloud resources.

Development fitting may begin only after these entry gates and a new protocol version freeze. It may select checkpoints using validation/selection data only. Confirmatory superiority requires the locked paired score test to pass Holm against **both** same-search controls and meet every V08 heterogeneity guardrail (game and color means at least -0.02, at most 5/20 checkpoint aggregates below -0.10, none below -0.25), with no differential censoring and no representation collapse. The primary inference is conditional on the finite frozen checkpoint panel. A pass supports only those checkpoints, the two named synthetic-data games, tested architectures, compute budget, and finite match schedule. It does not demonstrate broad training-seed generalization, game generalization, worst-case robustness, equilibrium quality, verified novelty, or Q1 acceptance.

## Explicitly deferred

Hidden-information, simultaneous-action, stochastic, and general-sum games remain outside scope. Exact-oracle results remain secondary and are not an entry gate for this match estimand. Third-party game data is not admitted. Cross-game transfer requires one shared encoder and identical weights; per-game encoders or checkpoints do not count as transfer.
